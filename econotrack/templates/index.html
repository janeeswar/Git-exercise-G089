Purpose of EconoTrack

EconoTrack is a global economic intelligence platform that helps users understand how commodity prices, economic news, and global events can affect daily life, business costs, and student expenses.

The system focuses on oil and gold trends, market comparisons, economic news, event impact analysis, student budget impact, saved articles, and price alert features.
